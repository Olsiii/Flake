# Cookie Policy — Flake Real Estate

**Last updated: 14.08.2026**

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They let the site remember information between page loads or visits — such as keeping you logged in, remembering your search filters, or recognizing repeat visits. Similar technologies (like browser local storage) work the same way and are covered by this policy too.

## 2. Categories of Cookies We Use

| Category | Purpose | Can you disable it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Keep you logged in, remember items in a saved search or draft form, maintain map/filter state as you browse, protect against fraud/abuse | No — required for the Platform to function |
| **Functional** | Remember preferences (e.g., last-viewed city, dismissed banners) | Yes, via cookie settings |
| **Analytics** | Understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, navigation paths) so we can improve it | Yes, via cookie settings |
| **Marketing** | Measure ad performance and, if we run campaigns, show relevant ads | Yes, via cookie settings |

At launch, Flake primarily uses **strictly necessary** cookies (for login and saved-search/filter state). We will update this table with specific cookie names and providers if/when we add analytics or marketing tools.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

Some features rely on third-party providers that may set their own cookies, including:
- Our authentication/database provider (keeps you logged in)
- Our mapping provider (may store map preferences)
- Any analytics or advertising tool we add in the future

These providers have their own privacy and cookie practices, which we encourage you to review if you'd like more detail.

## 4. Managing Cookies

When you first visit the Platform, a cookie banner lets you accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customize your choices by category. You can change your preferences at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the site footer. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent login and saved-search features from working.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as we add new features or tools. Material changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
